🎬 Demo Script

Your 30-minute step-by-step playbook for turning prospects into customers.

⏱️ Pre-Demo Checklist (5 min before)

Log in as Admin → demo-admin@clubforgehq.com / ClubForge2026!
Open incognito tab as Member → demo-member@clubforgehq.com / ClubForge2026!
Confirm demo data looks fresh (7 members, 5 classes, belt progressions)
Close all unrelated browser tabs
Have the prospect's club name, size, and discipline written down
1

The Hook

2 min
Thanks for making time, [Name]. Before I show you anything — quick question: how many hours a week would you say you spend on admin? Chasing payments, updating spreadsheets, answering WhatsApp messages about class times?

Wait for their answer. Most say 8–15 hours. Use their number.

That's about [X] hours you're not coaching, not growing your club, and not getting paid for. ClubForge was built to give you those hours back. Let me show you exactly how.
We built this for club owners like you — people who are amazing coaches but never signed up to be accountants and customer service reps. What you're about to see isn't another tool. It's your club's operating system.
Goal: Make them feel understood. They should be nodding before you show a single screen.
2

The Command Centre

5 min
Navigate to /admin — the Admin Dashboard
This is your command centre. Everything that matters about your club — in one glance.
Point At What to Say
Total Members "247 members across all your programmes — adults, kids, trial members — segmented and searchable."
Monthly Revenue "£8,420 this month. Not a guess — it pulls directly from your live Stripe data."
Attendance Rate "87% attendance rate. You'll know instantly if a class is dying or a member is drifting."
Classes This Week "18 classes running this week, 3 today. Your schedule, live."

"Tell me — right now, without looking anything up, do you know exactly how much revenue your club made last month? Or which members haven't shown up in 3 weeks? This is what ClubForge gives you from day one."

3

Member Management

5 min
Navigate to /admin/members — scroll the list, click a profile, highlight family accounts
Let's look at your members. Every profile, every membership, every grading — all in one place.
Navigate to /admin/invite — show the branded registration link
Getting new members on board? You share this link. They register, accept the waiver, pick their membership, and pay — all without you touching a thing. No paper forms. No chasing bank transfers.

"Think about the last time you onboarded a new member. How many texts did it take? How many days before they actually paid? With ClubForge, it's one link, five minutes, done."

4

Classes & Attendance

4 min
Navigate to /admin/classes, then /admin/class-roster, then /admin/attendance
Here's your class timetable. Every class, every instructor, every capacity limit. Click into any class and you see exactly who's checked in and how full you are.

"Imagine knowing, before the month ends, that 12 members haven't trained in 3 weeks. That's 12 cancellations you can prevent with one message. ClubForge makes that invisible pattern visible."

5

Billing & Finance

5 min
Navigate to /admin/finance, then /admin/membership-types, then /admin/promo-codes
Money. The thing nobody wants to talk about but everyone needs to manage. You set up your tiers — Adults, Kids, All-Access — Stripe handles the rest. Recurring billing, invoicing, failed payment recovery — all automatic.
Running a Black Friday deal? Create a promo code in 10 seconds. It integrates directly with Stripe.

"How much revenue are you losing right now to failed payments, expired cards, and members who just 'forget' to pay? Stripe handles all of that automatically — retry logic, card updates, dunning emails. You just see the money arrive."

6

Belt Progression

4 min
Navigate to /admin/grading-settings — show adult and kids belt schemes
This is where ClubForge really separates from generic gym software. Adult belts, kids belts with a 13-rank progression, professor access controls, grading feedback — all built in.

"Your members can see their belt history, grading feedback, and what they need to work on — from their phone. No more 'Sensei, when's my next grading?' messages."

Here's why this matters commercially: a member who can visualize their progress is a member who stays. Belt tracking turns abstract loyalty into visible momentum.
7

The Member Experience

3 min
Switch to the Member incognito tab — navigate to /dashboard
Now let me show you what YOUR members see. Sarah logged in — she can see her classes, belt progress, attendance streak, announcements. Clean, branded, mobile-friendly.
Screen What to Say
Dashboard home "One dashboard. Their entire club life."
Class schedule "Full timetable, check availability, one-tap check-in."
Belt progress "Their rank, stripes, and grading history — all visible."
Announcements "You post an announcement, it's here instantly. No WhatsApp spam."

"This is what your members will show their friends. 'Look at my club's app — I can check into class from my phone.' That's organic marketing that costs you nothing."

8

White-Label Branding

2 min
Navigate to /admin/settings — show colours, logo, tenant branding
Everything your members see is branded to you. Your club name, your colours, your logo. When they log in, they see YOUR club — not ClubForge. We just power it.

✅ Do

  • Ask about their setup first
  • Use their club name in examples
  • Pause after wow moments
  • Show admin AND member views
  • Relate features to their pain
  • Follow up within 2 hours

❌ Don't

  • Jump straight into features
  • Call it "the demo tenant"
  • Rush through every screen
  • Only show the admin side
  • List features like a brochure
  • End with "any questions?" only
