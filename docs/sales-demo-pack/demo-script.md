# 🎬 ClubForge Live Demo Script

> **Purpose**: A step-by-step, 30-minute guided walkthrough that turns prospects into customers.  
> **Tone**: Confident, conversational, never salesy. You're a fellow club owner who found the answer.  
> **Golden Rule**: *Show, don't tell.* Every claim is backed by a live click.

---

## Pre-Demo Checklist (5 min before)

- [ ] Log in as **Admin** → `demo-admin@clubforgehq.com` / `ClubForge2026!`
- [ ] Open a second incognito tab as **Member** → `demo-member@clubforgehq.com` / `ClubForge2026!`
- [ ] Tenant: **Apex MMA Academy** (slug: `apex-mma`)
- [ ] Confirm demo data looks fresh (7 members, 5 classes, attendance, belt progressions)
- [ ] Close all unrelated browser tabs — keep it clean and professional
- [ ] Have the prospect's **club name, size, and discipline** written down

## Act 1 — The Hook (2 min)

### Opening Line

> *"Thanks for making time, [Name]. Before I show you anything — quick question: how many hours a week would you say you spend on admin? Chasing payments, updating spreadsheets, answering WhatsApp messages about class times?"*

**Wait for their answer.** Most say 8–15 hours. Use their number.

> *"That's about [X] hours you're not coaching, not growing your club, and not getting paid for. ClubForge was built to give you those hours back. Let me show you exactly how."*

### Emotional Frame

> *"We built this for club owners like you — people who are amazing coaches but never signed up to be accountants and customer service reps. What you're about to see isn't another tool. It's your club's operating system."*

**🎯 Goal**: Make them feel understood. They should be nodding.

## Act 3 — Member Management (5 min)

### Members Page → `/admin/members`

> *"Let's look at your members. Every profile, every membership, every grading — all in one place."*

**Actions to demo:**
1. **Scroll the member list** — show the clean, searchable table
2. **Click a member profile** — show their details, belt rank, membership status
3. **Highlight family accounts** — *"Parents manage all their children from one login. One family, one account, zero confusion."*

### Invite Flow → `/admin/invite`

> *"Getting new members on board? Here's how easy it is."*

1. Show the invite page
2. Highlight the branded registration link
3. *"You share this link. They register, accept the waiver, pick their membership, and pay — all without you touching a thing. No paper forms. No chasing bank transfers."*

### 💥 Wow Moment #2

> *"Think about the last time you onboarded a new member. How many texts did it take? How many days before they actually paid? With ClubForge, it's one link, five minutes, done."*

## Demo Do's and Don'ts

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Ask about their current setup first | Jump straight into features |
| Use their club name in examples | Call it "the demo tenant" |
| Pause for reactions after wow moments | Rush through every screen |
| Show both admin AND member views | Only show the admin side |
| Relate features to their specific pain | List features like a brochure |
| Have the close ready | End with "any questions?" and nothing else |
| Follow up within 2 hours | Wait 3 days to send an email |
