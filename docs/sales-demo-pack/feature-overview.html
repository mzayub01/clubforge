⚡ Feature Overview

Everything ClubForge does — at a glance. Share this with prospects as a leave-behind.

🏋️

Member Management

  • Searchable member directory with role, belt rank, and membership status
  • Family accounts — parents manage all children from one login
  • Self-registration with branded sign-up link, waiver, and payment
  • One-click email invitations with branded onboarding
  • CSV/JSON data export for offline analysis or migration
📅

Class Scheduling & Attendance

  • Recurring and one-off classes with day, time, instructor, and capacity
  • Class roster — see who's enrolled, checked in, or absent
  • One-tap member check-in from any phone
  • Attendance analytics — spot trends, fading members, packed classes
  • Instructor assignment to specific classes and time slots
💳

Billing & Payments (Stripe)

  • Recurring subscriptions — set tiers, Stripe handles the rest
  • Automatic invoicing with real-time webhook status updates
  • Promo codes created in seconds, tied directly to Stripe
  • Finance dashboard — revenue overview, pending invoices, subscription statuses
  • Failed payment recovery — Stripe auto-retry and dunning emails

Belt Progression & Grading

  • Adult belt scheme — White → Blue → Purple → Brown → Black (with stripes)
  • Kids belt scheme — 13-belt progression for younger students
  • Full grading history with date, stripe count, and who awarded it
  • Professor feedback — written grading notes visible to the member
  • Professor access controls — class-specific promotion authority

Dashboard & Reports

  • KPI cards — total members, monthly revenue, attendance rate, classes
  • Basic reports — counts, attendance summaries, revenue (all tiers)
  • Advanced analytics — retention, forecasting, deep breakdowns Pro+
  • Data export — CSV, JSON, and API access by tier

Communication & Engagement

  • Announcements visible on every member's dashboard
  • Branded email templates Pro+
  • Event management with payments Pro+
  • Video library for technique drills Pro+
  • Waitlist management for oversubscribed classes Pro+

White-Label Branding

  • Branded member portal — members see YOUR club, not ClubForge

  • Custom waiver, etiquette, and registration messages

Multi-Location Support

  • Multiple venues from one dashboard (up to 3 Pro, unlimited Elite)
  • Location-specific classes, memberships, and announcements
  • Members can hold memberships at multiple sites
🔒

Security & Compliance

  • Enterprise PostgreSQL with row-level security
  • Stripe PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment processing
  • GDPR compliant — data export, deletion, consent mangement
  • Role-based access — admin, instructor, professor, member
