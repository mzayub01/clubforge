# ClubForge — Feature Overview

> The all-in-one operating system for gyms, dojos, and academies.  
> **One platform. Zero duct tape.**

---

## 🏋️ Member Management

Centralized profiles for every member — adults, kids, families.

- **Searchable member directory** with role, belt rank, and membership status
- **Family accounts** — parents manage all children from a single login
- **Self-registration** — branded sign-up link handles registration, waiver acceptance, and payment
- **Invite system** — one-click email invitations with branded onboarding
- **Data export** — CSV/JSON downloads for offline analysis or migration

---

## 📅 Class Scheduling & Attendance

Your entire timetable, live and connected.

- **Recurring and one-off classes** with day, time, instructor, and capacity
- **Class roster** — see who's enrolled, who checked in, who's absent
- **One-tap member check-in** — members check in from their phone
- **Attendance analytics** — spot trends, fading members, and packed classes at a glance
- **Instructor assignment** — allocate coaches to specific classes and time slots

---

## 💳 Billing & Payments (Stripe)

Get paid automatically. Chase nothing.

- **Recurring subscriptions** — set up membership tiers, Stripe handles the rest
- **Automatic invoicing** — real-time status updates via Stripe webhooks
- **Promo codes** — create discount codes in seconds, tied directly to Stripe
- **Finance dashboard** — revenue overview, pending invoices, subscription statuses
- **Failed payment recovery** — Stripe's retry logic and dunning emails recover revenue you'd normally lose

---

## 🥋 Belt Progression & Grading

The feature that makes club-specific software irreplaceable.

- **Adult belt scheme** — White → Blue → Purple → Brown → Black (with stripes)
- **Kids belt scheme** — 13-belt progression designed for younger students
- **Grading history** — every promotion tracked with date, stripe, and who awarded it
- **Professor feedback** — coaches leave written grading feedback visible to the member
- **Professor access controls** — admins grant class-specific promotion authority

---

## 📊 Admin Dashboard & Reports

Your club's vital signs, always visible.

- **KPI cards** — total members, monthly revenue, attendance rate, classes this week
- **Basic reports** — member counts, attendance summaries, revenue overview (all tiers)
- **Advanced analytics** — retention trends, revenue forecasting, detailed breakdowns (Pro+)
- **Data export** — CSV, JSON, and API access for deeper analysis (by tier)

---

## 📢 Communication & Engagement

Keep your community connected without the group chat chaos.

- **Announcements** — post updates visible on every member's dashboard
- **Email templates** — branded email communications (Pro+)
- **Event management** — create, manage, and take payments for seminars and competitions (Pro+)
- **Video library** — share technique videos and training drills with members (Pro+)
- **Waitlist management** — manage oversubscribed classes or new member queues (Pro+)

---

## 🎨 White-Label Branding

Your brand, your platform. Not ours.

- **Custom colours and branding** — your logo, your palette, your identity
- **Branded member portal** — members see YOUR club, not ClubForge
- **Custom subdomain** — `yourclub.clubforgehq.com` (Elite)
- **Custom waiver and etiquette text** — tailored to your club's culture
- **Custom registration messages** — set the tone from the first interaction

---

## 🏢 Multi-Location Support

One dashboard. Every venue.

- **Multiple locations per account** — up to 3 on Pro, unlimited on Elite
- **Location-specific classes, memberships, and announcements**
- **Members can hold memberships at multiple sites**
- **Per-location capacity and configuration**

---

## 🔒 Security & Compliance

Enterprise-grade, built in from day one.

- **Supabase (PostgreSQL)** — row-level security isolates every tenant's data
- **Stripe-secured payments** — PCI-DSS compliant payment processing
- **GDPR compliant** — data export, deletion, and consent management
- **Role-based access** — admin, instructor, professor, and member roles
