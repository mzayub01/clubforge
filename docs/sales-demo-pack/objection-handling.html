🛡️ Objection Handling

9 common objections with ready-made responses. Keep this open during every sales call.

1
"We already use spreadsheets / WhatsApp / pen and paper"
They're saying: "Our current system works well enough."
▼
"I totally get it — every club starts there. It works… until it doesn't. The problem with spreadsheets is you don't see what you're losing. You can't see the members who quietly drifted away, the payments that silently failed, or the 12 hours a week you spend on admin instead of coaching.

ClubForge doesn't replace your coaching — it replaces the 4-5 tools you're duct-taping together. One platform, everything connected. Most owners tell us they save 8-10 hours per week in the first month."

"Club owners typically spend 12+ hours/week on admin. ClubForge gets that to near-zero."

2
"It's too expensive"
They're saying: "I don't see the ROI yet."
▼
"I hear you — every pound matters. Let me put it this way: how much do you lose each month to members who cancel because they felt disconnected? Or payments that failed and never got chased?

Most clubs recover the cost in the first month just by reducing payment failures. Stripe's automated retry recovers revenue you're currently losing silently.

At £39/month for Starter — that's less than one membership. If ClubForge prevents even one cancellation per month, it pays for itself four times over."

"26 pence per member per month. That's less than the cost of one member's water bottle."

3
"My members won't use it"
They're saying: "I'm worried about adoption."
▼
"That's actually the best part — there's nothing to install. It's a web app that works on any phone. They tap a link, log in, done.

The member portal is simpler than WhatsApp. Check schedule, tap to check in, see belt progress. So simple that the 8-year-olds in our demo club use it.

The real question: what are your members using right now? WhatsApp for schedules? Texts for payment reminders? ClubForge gives them something better — a branded portal that makes your club look professional."

"Members don't adopt tools. They adopt experiences they enjoy. A member who can see their belt journey is a member who's engaged."

4
"We're too small for this"
They're saying: "We're not sure we're ready."
▼
"Actually, that's exactly when you should start. The clubs that struggle most are the ones that try to implement systems when they're already at 200 members and drowning.

Starter is designed for clubs under 150 members. £39/month — less than a pair of boxing gloves. And when you grow, everything scales with you."

"You're at the perfect stage. Building on the right foundation now means you'll never have to migrate or rebuild later."

5
"What about data security?"
They're saying: "Can I trust you with my members' data?"
▼
"Great question — more secure than a spreadsheet on your laptop.

Built on Supabase: enterprise PostgreSQL with row-level security. Every club's data is completely isolated. Payments through Stripe directly — PCI-DSS Level 1. We never see card numbers. GDPR compliant with full data export and deletion."

"Enterprise security, not enterprise pricing. ✅ Supabase RLS • ✅ Stripe PCI-DSS • ✅ GDPR • ✅ Full data isolation"

6
"Can I try before I commit?"
They're saying: "I'm interested but nervous."
▼
"Absolutely. 14 days, full Pro features, no credit card required. Set up your real club, invite a few members, see how it feels.

After 14 days, if it's not for you, your account simply pauses. No charges, no pressure. We don't even take a card upfront.

Most people know by day 3. Once you see your dashboard with your real members and classes, it clicks."

"Let's get you set up right now — takes 10 minutes. 👉 clubforgehq.com/get-started"

7
"I've tried gym software before and it was terrible"
They're saying: "I've been burned and I'm skeptical."
▼
"I hear that a lot. Most gym software is built for big-box fitness chains — treadmill bookings and smoothie bars. Not for you.

ClubForge is built specifically for martial arts. Belt progression, kids schemes, professor access, grading feedback — you won't find that in MindBody or GymMaster.

And the trial is free. No contract, no setup fee, no card. The worst that happens is you waste 10 minutes."

"Generic gym software doesn't know what a purple belt is. We do."

8
"I need to talk to my partner / co-owner"
They're saying: "I need backup to make this decision."
▼
"Of course — smart move. Would it help if I sent a quick summary they can review? I can include the pricing, features, and a trial link so they can poke around themselves.

Better yet — when would be a good time for all of us to jump on a quick 15-minute call?"

Send the Feature Overview + Pricing docs. Book a follow-up within 3 days. Don't let this go cold.

9
"Can you integrate with [X]?"
They're saying: "I have a workflow I don't want to lose."
▼
"We integrate natively with Stripe for payments. Elite plan offers full API access and webhooks.

But honestly — most clubs find they don't need integrations because ClubForge already handles what they were using 4-5 separate tools for. What specifically are you looking to connect?"

Email → "Built-in announcements + templates." Scheduling → "Full class scheduling included." Accounting → "Stripe + CSV export."

"That's a fair point. Let me make a note of that and get you a proper answer rather than guessing. In the meantime — the trial is free and there's zero commitment. Why don't you take it for a spin while I dig into that for you?"
