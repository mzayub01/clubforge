# 🛡️ Objection Handling — Cheat Sheet

> Every objection is a buying signal in disguise. They're not saying no — they're asking *"convince me."*

---

## 1. "We already use spreadsheets / WhatsApp / pen and paper"

**What they're really saying**: *"Our current system works well enough."*

### Response:

> *"I totally get it — every club starts there. It works… until it doesn't. The problem with spreadsheets is you don't see what you're losing. You can't see the members who quietly drifted away, the payments that silently failed, or the 12 hours a week you spend on admin instead of coaching.*
>
> *ClubForge doesn't replace your coaching — it replaces the 4-5 tools you're duct-taping together. One platform, everything connected. Most owners tell us they save 8-10 hours per week in the first month."*

### Power stat:
> *"Club owners typically spend 12+ hours/week on admin and juggle 4-5 different tools. ClubForge gets that to near-zero."*

---

## 2. "It's too expensive"

**What they're really saying**: *"I don't see the ROI yet."*

### Response:

> *"I hear you — every pound matters when you're running a club. Let me put it this way: how much do you lose each month to members who cancel because they felt disconnected? Or payments that failed and never got chased?*
>
> *Most clubs recover the cost of ClubForge in the first month just by reducing payment failures alone. Stripe's automated retry and dunning recovers revenue you're currently losing silently.*
>
> *And at £39/month for Starter — that's less than one membership. If ClubForge prevents even one cancellation per month, it pays for itself four times over."*

### Price anchors:
| | Monthly | Daily | Per Member (150) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | £39 | £1.30 | 26p |
| **Pro** | £129 | £4.30 | 17p |

> *"26 pence per member per month. That's the cost of running a professional club."*

---

## 3. "My members won't use it"

**What they're really saying**: *"I'm worried about adoption."*

### Response:

> *"That's actually the best part — there's nothing for them to install. It's a web app that works on any phone. They tap a link, log in, and they're in.*
>
> *And the member portal is designed to be simpler than WhatsApp. They check their class schedule, tap to check in, and see their belt progress. It's so simple that the 8-year-olds in our demo club use it.*
>
> *The real question is: what are your members using right now? WhatsApp for schedules? Texts for payment reminders? ClubForge gives them something better — a branded portal that makes your club look professional and organized."*

### Clincher:
> *"Members don't adopt tools. They adopt experiences they enjoy. A member who can see their belt journey and streak count is a member who's engaged."*

---

## 4. "We're too small for this"

**What they're really saying**: *"We're not sure we're ready."*

### Response:

> *"Actually, that's exactly when you should start. The clubs that struggle most are the ones that try to implement systems when they're already at 200 members and drowning.*
>
> *ClubForge Starter is designed for clubs with under 150 members. It's £39/month — less than a pair of boxing gloves. And when you grow (because you will), everything scales with you. Your data, your member history, your class records — it all carries forward.*
>
> *Think of it like this: would you rather organize your house when it's tidy, or after it's a mess?"*

### Reframe:
> *"You're not too small. You're at the perfect stage. Building on the right foundation now means you'll never have to migrate, re-enter data, or rebuild later."*

---

## 5. "What about data security?"

**What they're really saying**: *"Can I trust you with my members' data?"*

### Response:

> *"Great question — and the answer is: more secure than a spreadsheet on your laptop.*
>
> *ClubForge is built on Supabase, which is enterprise-grade PostgreSQL with row-level security. That means every club's data is completely isolated — your data can never mix with another club's.*
>
> *Payments go through Stripe directly — we never see or store card numbers. Stripe is PCI-DSS Level 1 certified, which is the highest security standard in the payments industry.*
>
> *And we're GDPR compliant. Members can request data export or deletion at any time. You can actually export your data as CSV whenever you need it."*

### Trust points:
- ✅ Supabase (enterprise PostgreSQL) with row-level security (RLS)
- ✅ Stripe PCI-DSS Level 1 for payments
- ✅ GDPR compliant with data export & deletion
- ✅ Tenant data isolation — your club's data is completely separate
- ✅ No card details stored on our servers

### Response:

>
> *ClubForge is built specifically for martial arts clubs, dojos, and academies. That's why we have belt progression tracking, kids belt schemes, professor access controls, and grading feedback. You won't find that in MindBody or GymMaster.*
>
> *And the trial is free. No contract, no setup fee, no card. The worst that happens is you waste 10 minutes. The best that happens is you find the system you wish you'd had from day one."*

### Differentiator:
> *"Generic gym software doesn't know what a purple belt is. We do."*

## 8. "I need to talk to my partner / co-owner"

**What they're really saying**: *"I need backup to make this decision."*

### Response:

> *"Of course — that's a smart move. Would it help if I sent you a quick summary you can share with them? I can include the pricing, the features, and a link to the free trial so they can poke around themselves.*
>
> *Better yet — when would be a good time for all of us to jump on a quick 15-minute call? I'm happy to answer any questions they have directly."*

### Action:
- Send the [Feature Overview](./feature-overview.md) and [Pricing & ROI](./pricing-roi.md) docs
- Book a follow-up call within 3 days
- Don't let this go cold — follow up proactively

---

## 9. "Can you integrate with [X]?"

**What they're really saying**: *"I have a specific workflow I don't want to lose."*

### Response:

> *"We integrate natively with Stripe for all payments and billing — that's our core financial backbone. And on our Elite plan, we offer full API access and webhooks so you can connect ClubForge to anything.*
>
> *But honestly — most clubs find they don't need integrations because ClubForge already handles the things they were using 4-5 separate tools for. What specifically are you looking to connect?"*

### Follow up based on their answer:
- **Email marketing**: *"We have announcements and custom email templates built in."*
- **Scheduling**: *"Full class scheduling with roster management is included."*
- **Website**: *"Members access their portal via your branded subdomain."*
- **Accounting**: *"Stripe provides full transaction exports, and we have CSV data export."*

## The Universal Safety Net

If you get an objection you haven't prepared for:

> *"That's a fair point. Let me make a note of that and get you a proper answer rather than guessing. In the meantime — the trial is free and there's zero commitment. Why don't you take it for a spin while I dig into that for you?"*

**Never leave a conversation without a clear next step.**
