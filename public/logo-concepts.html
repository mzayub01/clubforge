⭐ Concept A — Recommended

The CF Monogram

A geometric interlock of C and F. The C arc wraps around the F's vertical stem — the club containing and supporting its structural backbone. The gold accent on the F's middle bar is the "forge point" — where components connect and value is created. This is the strongest concept: it scales from favicon to billboard, reads as a mark and initials simultaneously, and feels premium without being decorative.

Symbol Logic

C = Club (the container/community). F = Forge (the structural backbone). The C wraps around the F, showing a system that holds and supports. The gold bar = the forge point where value is created.

Why It's Strongest

Reads as initials and abstract mark simultaneously. Works at 16px favicon and 200px hero. The interlocking geometry implies infrastructure without being literal. Timeless — won't date.

Concept B

The Structural Foundation

Four blocks ascending — a foundation being built, layer by layer. The gold "keystone" block represents the forge, the critical connection point that makes the structure whole. This concept communicates infrastructure, building, and structured growth more literally than Concept A.

Symbol Logic

Stacking blocks = building something structured. The ascending form implies growth and scaling. The gold keystone = the forge, the critical piece that holds everything together.

Strengths & Trade-offs

Strong "building/structure" metaphor. Works well at larger sizes. At very small sizes (favicon), the 4 blocks may lose definition. More abstract than Concept A — less immediately identifiable as "CF".

Concept C

The Modern Shield

An abstract, modern shield form — NOT heraldic or medieval — split into two navy tones to create depth and dimension. A gold vertical "spine" runs through the centre as the backbone of the system. A subtle cross beam adds structural integrity. Communicates protection, reliability, and systematic strength.

Symbol Logic

Shield = protection and reliability. The two-tone split adds modern depth (not flat). The gold spine = the backbone of the system. Cross beam = structural integrity and connection.

Strengths & Trade-offs

Strong "protection/trust" feel. Instantly communicates reliability. Risk: shields can feel security-software or heraldic if not careful. This version is sufficiently abstract to avoid that.

Typography

Inter (800 weight) for the wordmark. Clean, geometric, modern SaaS standard. Alternatives: Outfit, General Sans, or Satoshi for a slightly warmer feel. Avoid decorative or serif fonts.

Usage Rules

Symbol can be used standalone (favicon, app icon). Full logo (symbol + wordmark) for headers and marketing. Gold accent should never be used as a background — only as an accent element. Monochrome: use pure black or pure white.
